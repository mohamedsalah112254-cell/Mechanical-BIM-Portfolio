// Research & Achievements

Graduation Project

Solar-Powered Reverse Osmosis Desalination Plant

Designed a seawater and well-water reverse osmosis desalination plant using an innovative pressure-exchanger configuration that recovers energy from the high-pressure pump discharge. Modeled the full plant to study how changes in input parameters affect performance, then sized a solar PV supply to offset most of the plant's power draw.

Awarded Best Project in the Fluid Division of the Mechanical Engineering Department (2022) and funded with a 75,000 EGP grant from the Egyptian Academy of Scientific Research and Technology. Supervised by Dr. Ihab Adam, Dr. Mohamed Sewdane and Eng. Mohamed Fouad.

5 m³/day Plant capacity
60% Pump energy recovered via pressure exchanger
80% Power draw offset by solar PV
75,000 EGP Research grant awarded
